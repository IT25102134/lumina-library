⚠ Deactivate your account

This action will deactivate your Lumina Library membership. You will be signed out immediately and will not be able to sign in until a librarian reactivates your account.

  • All your borrowing history, fines and reading list data will be preserved.
  • You cannot deactivate while you have active or overdue loans.
  • You cannot deactivate while you have pending borrow requests.
  • You cannot deactivate while you have unpaid fines.
